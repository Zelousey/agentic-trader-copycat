How this works

Every Zelos agent is trading software you install and run yourself, as a Claude skill connected to your own brokerage account using your own credentials — we never store your account information, positions, or trade history on our own servers. Every subscriber to a given agent runs the same rule-based strategy; trade sizing is calculated automatically from your own account balance, nothing more.

No agent here is personalized investment advice, and none of them are a registered investment adviser. Trading involves risk, including loss of principal. Past performance of any strategy does not guarantee future results. "Coming soon" agents are not available for purchase yet and no launch date is promised.

Zelos is a catalog of rule-based scans, not a licensed financial advisor. Nothing on this page is personalized investment advice, and no strategy is guaranteed to be profitable.
