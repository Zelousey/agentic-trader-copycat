What it's actually looking for

Four shapes on a chart. Nothing else counts.

Every candidate gets sorted into one of these — or thrown out. If a stock's chart doesn't look like one of these, it doesn't matter how exciting the ticker is.

Pullback in an uptrend

Climbing steadily, pauses, catches its breath near support.

Bull flag

A sharp run, then a tight, orderly drift sideways — not a collapse.

Breakout + retest

Breaks a level, comes back to prove that level now holds.

Higher-low reversal

A decline that stops short of its last low, then turns back up.

How it decides

Four checks, every session, before anything gets suggested.

01

Read the market

Checks whether stocks broadly are trending up, down, or sideways first — the bar for a good setup gets stricter when the backdrop is weak.

02

Scan for pullbacks, not runners

Screens hundreds of stocks for ones climbing steadily that have recently paused — then explicitly throws out anything that's already made its big move. Chasing is the most common way this kind of trade goes wrong.

03

Score it, don't just spot it

Trend, support, volume, reward-to-risk, relative strength — scored out of 80. Below the bar, it gets dropped, even if it "looks" interesting.

04

You decide, every time

If something clears the bar, the alert publishes with the entry, stop, target, and reward:risk laid out, plus a general risk-sizing rule of thumb — never a number calculated from your account, since Zelos never sees it. What you do with that information, in your own brokerage account, is entirely your call.

Trust & safety

The rules that don't bend.

✓

No account connection, ever. Zelos never asks for your brokerage credentials and never links to any account. There's nothing to authorize and nothing for it to touch — it publishes an alert page, full stop.

✓

Nothing here places a trade. Every run only researches and reports. If you decide to act on an alert, you place that trade yourself, in your own brokerage account, on your own judgment.

✓

Sizing is a general rule of thumb, not a calculation on your money. Because Zelos never sees your balance, it can't and doesn't size a position for you — the alert offers a general guideline (like risking 1–2% of an account per idea) for you to apply to your own situation.

✓

"No trade today" is a normal outcome. The bar exists to be missed most days — that's what keeps it a strategy instead of a habit.

✓

Nothing here is a guarantee. This approach hasn't been backtested against a long track record. It's rules, applied consistently — not a promise of returns.
