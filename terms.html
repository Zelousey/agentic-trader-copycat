01 What Zelos is

Zelos is a published, rule-based trade-alert page, not trading software you install, run, or connect to a brokerage account. It scans the market using a fixed strategy, scores candidate setups, and — when a setup qualifies — publishes an alert with an entry, stop, target, and reward:risk to a page kept current on a fixed schedule. Every subscriber sees the same alert, published the same way, at the same time.

Zelos never asks for your brokerage credentials, never connects to any account, and never places a trade on your behalf or anyone else's. If you decide to act on a published alert, you do so entirely on your own, in your own brokerage account, using your own judgment.
