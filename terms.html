01 What Zelos is

Zelos is software you install and run yourself under your own Claude account. It scans the market using a fixed, rule-based strategy, scores candidate setups, and — when a setup qualifies — presents you with an entry, stop, target, and suggested position size as an alert with Accept and Decline. Zelos always waits for you to approve a specific trade before it is placed; nothing is bought unless you tap Accept on that specific setup, and tapping Decline (or not responding) simply skips it.

Zelos connects to your brokerage account through your own credentials via your own account connection. Nobody at Zelos places trades on your behalf using a separate account of theirs — every order is submitted from your account, under your authorization, using the mode you selected.
