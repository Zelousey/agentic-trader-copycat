Getting started

Four steps to your first alert.

This is the whole setup — nothing to install, nothing to connect, no account required. Just find your link, bookmark it, and check back each session.

✅ You're in. Your Gumroad receipt has your alert link and a link back to this guide — the steps below get you from purchase to your first read.
01

Find your alert link

Your Gumroad receipt email has a direct link to your alert page — that's the one page you'll want to come back to. Can't find it? Sign in at gumroad.com with the email you purchased with, open Library, and it'll be right there next to Zelos.

This link is general information, published the same way to every subscriber — it isn't tied to any account, and Zelos never asks for one.
02

See a sample first

Open the sample alert ticket to see the exact format — ticker, entry, stop, targets, reward:risk — before your first real one shows up. It's a fixed example, not a live signal.

03

Bookmark your alert page

Your alert page updates in place, on the same schedule every session — the ticket you see there is always the current one. That's the page you want on your home screen, not this setup guide, and not the public Zelos site you bought from.

iPhone (Safari): open your alert link → tap the Share icon → Add to Home Screen → Add.
Android (Chrome): open the link → tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen / Install app.

04

Check it each session

Zelos runs the scan and republishes the page on a fixed schedule — there's nothing for you to trigger or configure. Most sessions won't turn up a qualifying setup, and that's the strategy working as intended, not a failed check. Whatever you decide to do with a published alert happens entirely in your own brokerage account, on your own.

How Zelos works

Zelos is a published research page, not trading software you install or connect to an account. It never asks for brokerage credentials, never sees your balance or positions, and never places an order. Every subscriber sees the same rule-based scan, on the same schedule — general information, not personalized to any individual's account or situation.
