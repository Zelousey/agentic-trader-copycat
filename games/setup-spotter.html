Got an eye for it?

Some rounds show a chart — tap Accept for a real setup (a pullback, a bull flag, a breakout retest, a higher low), Decline for noise, a downtrend, or something already too extended.

Other rounds show three candles and ask you to tap a specific one — Doji, Hammer, Shooting Star, Marubozu, Spinning Top. Get it right and we'll tell you why it's shaped that way.
